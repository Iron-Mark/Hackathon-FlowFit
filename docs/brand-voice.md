# FlowFit brand voice

How FlowFit sounds when it is at its best. Extracted from the live landing
page, the Flowy companion, and the README — not invented. When new copy
conflicts with this doc, match the surfaces quoted here.

## Brand personality

FlowFit is the encouraging friend who shows up to your first workout, not the
coach who counts your reps. Warm and a little playful through Flowy, but
grounded: it measures effort with real sensors and says plainly what works
where. It never pressures, never inflates, and never talks down to anyone —
kid or parent.

## Voice attributes

### Warm, not childish
- We are: friendly, encouraging, plain-spoken. Parents are the gatekeepers
  reading the page, so warmth never tips into baby talk.
- We are not: saccharine, hype-driven, or exclamation-heavy.
- This sounds like: "A simple loop for movement, recovery, and progress."
- This does NOT sound like: "The FUN-nest fitness adventure EVER!!!"

### Buddy-first
- We are: led by Flowy. The buddy is the interface; charts and metrics stay
  behind it. A buddy, not a dashboard.
- We are not: a stats dashboard with a mascot stapled on.
- This sounds like: "Companion progress, mood checks, and wellness missions
  give the app a warmer reason to come back."
- This does NOT sound like: "Advanced analytics dashboard with 12 KPIs."

### Sensor-honest
- We are: precise about what is measured and where it works. Effort is
  measured, not guessed. Spell out beat-to-beat intervals; never the bare
  acronym IBI on consumer surfaces.
- We are not: vague ("AI wellness magic") or absolute ("works on any watch").
- This sounds like: "Capture pulse and workout moments from Wear OS."
- This does NOT sound like: "Perfect accuracy on every device!"

### Try-it-first
- We are: low-pressure. The browser preview comes before any install ask.
- We are not: pushy about downloads, sign-ups, or upgrades.
- This sounds like: "Start fast in the browser, then move to Android and
  Wear OS when device-connected testing matters."
- This does NOT sound like: "Download NOW before it's too late!"

## Audience

- Primary: parents and adult gatekeepers deciding whether FlowFit enters the
  household. They care about honesty (what is sensed, what is stored), a calm
  tone, and an app that motivates without manipulating.
- Secondary: kids and fitness beginners who actually move with Flowy. They
  care about the buddy feeling alive, missions feeling like play, and
  encouragement that never scolds.

## Messaging pillars

1. A buddy, not a dashboard — beginners need encouragement without judgment.
   Flowy leads every surface; numbers support the buddy, never replace it.
2. Measured, not guessed — gatekeepers need honesty. Real heart-rate readings
   and beat-to-beat intervals via the Samsung Health Sensor SDK, described
   plainly and qualified where support varies.
3. Try it before you install it — evaluators need a no-risk first step. Web
   preview first, APK second, watch pairing when the device flow matters.
4. One loop across watch, phone, and browser — households need simplicity.
   Movement, recovery, and progress travel with the user across devices.

## Tone by surface

Voice stays fixed; only the temperature shifts.

| Surface | Tone shift |
| --- | --- |
| Landing page | Calm and benefit-led, written for parents. No exclamations. Buddy visible above the fold; charts behind it. |
| Store listing | The most factual register. Qualified claims only ("where supported"). See the note below before any kid-targeting language. |
| Release notes | Matter-of-fact: what changed and why it helps. No superlatives, no mascot theatrics. |
| In-app copy (Flowy) | The warmest register. Second person, contractions, at most one exclamation per message ("Hey there! Let's keep that energy going!"). |

Note on "for kids": FlowFit's unified story is kid-friendly fitness, and the
buddy carries it. Marketing pages may say kid-friendly. But "for kids" as a
target-audience claim in the store listing triggers Google Play Families
policy obligations, so that copy must go through the Families compliance
decision before any Play submission.

## Style rules

- Headings and labels use sentence case ("How it works", "Account deletion").
  Buttons and CTAs use title case ("Try Web App", "Download APK").
- Use contractions ("Let's", "it's"). Stiff copy reads as corporate, not buddy.
- Exclamation points: zero on landing, store, and release-note surfaces.
  Flowy may use one per message in-app, never stacked.
- Numbers are numerals with units spelled the way devices show them:
  "72 BPM synced", "42 active minutes". BPM is always uppercase.
- Hyphenate compound modifiers ("heart-rate tracking") but not the noun
  ("your heart rate").
- Avoid em dashes in consumer copy; the existing voice prefers a period and a
  new short sentence. One idea per sentence.
